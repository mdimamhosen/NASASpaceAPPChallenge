NASA Mars Trek distributes planetary map layers using OGC WMTS. Its Mars tile documentation describes equirectangular products, tile matrices, and service metadata. Mars Explorer displays the MGS MOLA color-shaded global mosaic as regional topographic context. The rendered colors are imagery, not a quantitative elevation profile for the user's route.
