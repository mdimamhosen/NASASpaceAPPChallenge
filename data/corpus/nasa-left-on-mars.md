# Left on Mars: NASA landers, rovers and the Ingenuity helicopter

NASA has landed eleven spacecraft on Mars: two Viking landers, the Mars Pathfinder lander and its Sojourner rover, the Spirit and Opportunity rovers, the Phoenix lander, the Curiosity rover, the InSight lander, the Perseverance rover and the Ingenuity helicopter. Curiosity and Perseverance are still active. The others went silent on the dates below, taken from each mission page on science.nasa.gov.

- **Viking 1 lander** (Viking 1, lander): landed 1976-07-20. Last contact 1982-11-11. Stopped operating after getting a faulty command. Source: https://science.nasa.gov/mission/viking-1/
- **Viking 2 lander** (Viking 2, lander): landed 1976-09-03. Last contact 1980-04-12. Continued to transmit data until April 12, 1980. Source: https://science.nasa.gov/mission/viking-2/
- **Carl Sagan Memorial Station (Pathfinder lander)** (Mars Pathfinder, lander): landed 1997-07-04. Last contact 1997-09-27. End of mission: Sept. 27, 1997. Source: https://science.nasa.gov/mission/mars-pathfinder/
- **Sojourner rover** (Mars Pathfinder, rover): landed 1997-07-04. Last contact 1997-09-27. Deployed by the Pathfinder lander; the mission ended Sept. 27, 1997. Source: https://science.nasa.gov/mission/mars-pathfinder/
- **Spirit rover** (Mars Exploration Rover (Spirit), rover): landed 2004-01-03. Last contact 2010-03-22. Became stuck in soft soil on May 1, 2009; no contact after March 22, 2010; NASA declared the mission complete on May 25, 2011. Source: https://science.nasa.gov/mission/mer-spirit/
- **Opportunity rover** (Mars Exploration Rover (Opportunity), rover): landed 2004-01-24. Last contact 2018-06-10. Stopped communicating after a severe Mars-wide dust storm blanketed its location; last signal June 10, 2018. Source: https://science.nasa.gov/mission/mer-opportunity/
- **Phoenix lander** (Phoenix, lander): landed 2008-05-25. Last contact 2008-11-02. End of mission: Nov. 2, 2008, in the arctic plains of Vastitas Borealis. Source: https://science.nasa.gov/mission/phoenix/
- **Curiosity rover** (Mars Science Laboratory, rover): landed 2012-08-06. Still active. Source: https://science.nasa.gov/mission/msl-curiosity/
- **InSight lander** (InSight, lander): landed 2018-11-26. Last contact 2022-12-15. End of mission: Dec. 15, 2022. Source: https://science.nasa.gov/mission/insight/
- **Perseverance rover** (Mars 2020, rover): landed 2021-02-18. Still active. Source: https://science.nasa.gov/mission/mars-2020-perseverance/
- **Ingenuity helicopter** (Mars 2020, helicopter): landed 2021-02-18. Last contact 2024-01-18. Flew for the last time on Jan. 18, 2024, and completed its mission on Jan. 25, 2024, after 72 flights. Source: https://science.nasa.gov/mission/mars-2020-perseverance/ingenuity-mars-helicopter/

Mars Explorer shows these on its Mars map as the Left on Mars layer with a last-contact timeline. Rovers that drove away are drawn at their landing site, because their final positions are not mapped in this app; Perseverance is drawn at its latest published PDS PLACES localization.
