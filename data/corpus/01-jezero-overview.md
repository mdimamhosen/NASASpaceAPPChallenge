NASA selected Jezero Crater as the landing site for the Mars 2020 Perseverance rover. The mission investigates whether the region could have supported ancient microbial life, characterizes its geology and past climate, and collects samples for possible future return to Earth. Jezero's geological context makes the region scientifically relevant, but map annotations in this demo do not represent validated rover stops. Terrain scores in this app are heuristics and are not safety assessments.
